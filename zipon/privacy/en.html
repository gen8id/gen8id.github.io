Privacy Policy

Zipon (집온)
Effective date: 2026-11-23

This Privacy Policy explains how Zipon / 집온 (the "App"), provided by Generated, Inc. ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: the App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The App is free, contains no ads, does not request internet access, and everything it stores stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no backend servers, and no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. The App is an offline archive (compressed file) extractor for Android.

2. Information stored only on your device

To provide its features, the App may store the following in its private storage on your device. This information is never sent to us.

DataPurpose
Display settings such as themeRemembers your display preferences
Recent archives list (file reference, name, time, and similar) Makes it easier to reopen recent files
Last folder used in the file picker Opens the picker in the same folder next time

When you open an archive, the App accesses it only with the permission you grant through the Android file picker or by opening the file from another app. Extracted files are written only to the location you choose.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.

Android backup: if you have enabled Android device backup, Android may include the App's settings in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This is handled by Android and your backup provider under your own settings, not by us.

3. Permissions

The App typically does not request internet permission, and does not request location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions. Files and folders are accessed only through mechanisms such as the Android Storage Access Framework for items you choose.

4. Features that involve other apps or services

  • Open / save files: reads or writes only at locations you choose.
  • Share: when you choose Share, the App may hand a file to another app you select. What that app does with the file is governed by its own privacy policy.

If the App opens a website in your browser, that site is operated under its own privacy policy. Archive contents and on-device data from the App are not sent automatically.

5. Sharing and sale of data

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

7. Security

Data the App stores is kept in its private app storage, which other apps cannot access under Android's security model.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date.
